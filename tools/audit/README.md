# Audit tools (read-only; run from repo root with `.venv/bin/python`)
- `tools/audit/run_all.py [--last 8] [--save-baseline [PATH]] [--skip-perf]` → `context/metrics/latest.{md,json}` + guard deltas vs `baseline.json`.
- `tools/audit/eval_matches.py --checkpoint-dir DIR [--code-root PATH] [--matches 32] [--seed 1234] [--out JSON] [--keep-replays DIR]` → N fresh, unselected matches from a checkpoint (never writes checkpoints/replays) with per-match metrics and guard mean/se/n.
- `run_all.py --eval-checkpoint DIR [--eval-code-root PATH] [--eval-matches 32] [--save-baseline-unbiased]` → replay guards from fresh matches, compared with `baseline_unbiased.json`. Prefer this over `./replays` whenever training used `--save-best-only`.
- `tools/audit/harness.py --checkpoint-dir DIR --opp {self|scripted|scripted2|frozen:DIR} --mode {sampled|mixed|greedy} --n 96 --seed 11 --workers 10 --out-dir OUT [--sigma-scale S] [--swap] [--store-obs] [--breakdown]` → seeded full matches written as step-resolution `.npz` traces (checkpoint read-only; refuses to write inside any checkpoint dir or `checkpoints/`/`replays/`). Match `i` is identical for any `n`/`--workers`.
  - Policy heads: each team's head is read from the output width of its actor (4 outputs = `gaussian4`, 5 = `hybrid5`, 6 = `hybrid6`), so the heads can play each other (`--opp frozen:DIR`, `--swap`). The trace field `head` is an `(2,)` int8 array `[team A, team B]` per match: `0` = `gaussian4` (or a scripted team), `1` = `hybrid5`, `2` = `hybrid6`. `--swap` gives team A the `red_*` policies and team B the `blue_*` ones, and `head` follows the seats. `internals.py` reads `head` (absent on older traces means `[0, 0]`).
  - Modes for a `hybrid5` team: `sampled` draws throttle, steer, kick power and both fires; `mixed` uses the deterministic means for throttle, steer and kick power and samples only the two fires (kick, special); `greedy` uses the means and fires when the fire probability exceeds 0.5. `--sigma-scale` scales the three Gaussian components only, never the fires. For a `gaussian4` team `mixed` keeps throttle and steer at their means and samples kick and special.
  - A `hybrid6` team is a `hybrid5` team with a fourth Gaussian, the aim: a seat whose observation column 7 says it holds the ball and that fires the kick takes the aim as its env steer (the kick deflection); every other seat steers with the steer Gaussian. It plays the same modes (`mixed` uses the aim mean like the steer mean), and `--sigma-scale` scales its four Gaussians. The trace field `aim_mean` `(T, 12)` float32, written only for matches in which a team plays `hybrid6`, holds `tanh(z_aim)` on `hybrid6` seats and NaN on every other seat.
  - Trace rows are env-space `(12, 4)` arrays for every head. For a `hybrid5` team `act` is the decoded env action (kick `-1` when not fired, else `0.35 + 0.65 * power`; special `+1`/`-1`) and `mean` is `[tanh(z_throttle), tanh(z_steer), 2 * p_kick - 1, 2 * p_special - 1]`.
- `tools/audit/census.py TRACE_DIR [TRACE_DIR ...] [--json OUT]` → behaviour metrics over harness traces (goals and goal differential, possession, holder spells, kick outcomes, kick propensity by team/role, `n_within(R)`, clusters, role inversion, per-keeper geometry with fixed 195 u box, keeper opportunities, goal types, and early-vs-late last-225-decisions time-binned metrics) as mean +- SE with n. Metric definitions are in its module docstring. The scripted opponent is `football_rl/baselines.py`.
  - Pass options at the kick (outfield kicks, both teams pooled, from `pos[k]`, `ball[k]` and `bvel[k + 1]`; a kick on the last row enters no key): a candidate is an outfield teammate 80-700 u from the ball (inclusive); its lane is open when every opponent (keeper included) projects outside the open interval (0, length) of the segment ball -> teammate or lies strictly more than 50 u from it; a kick aims at a candidate when its direction `bvel[k + 1]` is within 15 deg (inclusive) of ball -> teammate, and has no direction below 50 u/s. `pass_option_share` = kicks with an open candidate / kicks; `aim_open_share` = kicks aimed at an open candidate / kicks with one and a direction; `aim_open_completion` = those aimed kicks with `kick_outcome` `teammate` / aimed kicks with an outcome; `no_option_opponent_share` = kicks without an open candidate with outcome `opponent` / such kicks with an outcome. Pooled ratios with the cluster-robust SE, like `kick_outcome_*`.
  - Chance baselines for the aim (same kicks and open set; kicks with an open candidate and a direction; facing = `ang[k, i]`, the kicker's body angle at the kick row; angles wrapped to (-pi, pi]; a trace without `ang` enters none of these keys): `open_reachable_share` = kicks with an open candidate within 50 deg of the facing (35 deg of kick deflection plus the 15 deg aim cone) / kicks with an open candidate and a direction; `aim_open_reachable_share` = kicks aimed at an open candidate among those reachable kicks / reachable kicks; `aim_open_arc_baseline` = mean over the reachable kicks of the share of the 71 angles facing + {-35, -34, ..., +35} deg within 15 deg of an open candidate, i.e. the aim share a uniformly random direction in the kickable arc would score. Compare `aim_open_reachable_share` with `aim_open_arc_baseline`. The report's `pass options` line ends with `reachable`, `aim|reach` and `arc baseline`.
  - Basin keys (outfield seats only, per team A / B and pooled as `<key>_A`, `<key>_B`, `<key>_pooled`; pooled ratios of per-match counts with the cluster-robust SE; a trace without `ang` or without `vel` enters none of them, and a player-decision with a non-finite value among the quantities a key reads is skipped): `reverse_motion_share` = outfield player-decisions with speed > 40 u/s and cos(`vel`, facing `ang`) < -0.5 (the body moves within 60 deg of straight backwards) / all outfield player-decisions; `facing_ball_share` = outfield player-decisions whose bearing to the ball is within 60 deg (inclusive, wrapped) of the facing / all outfield player-decisions (a player exactly on the ball counts as facing it); `tackle_ready_share` = over decisions in which an outfield player holds the ball, the opposing outfield players within 85 u (inclusive) of the carrier that face it, cos(facing, carrier - tackler) >= 0.25 (the facing gate of a tackle), / all opposing outfield players within 85 u (the team is the tacklers' team; loose-ball and keeper-held decisions enter nothing). The report line is `basin: reverse X | facing ball Y | tackle-ready Z` (pooled). A seed that walks backwards (the reverse-walking basin) shows a high reverse share, a low facing share and a low tackle-ready share.
- `tools/audit/internals.py TRACE_DIR [TRACE_DIR ...] [--json OUT]` → policy-internal metrics over harness traces (head saturation `sat99_<head>`, local tanh gradient scale `grad_scale_<head>`, eligible kick decomposition `p_kick_obs`/`p_kick_intent`/`kick_from_intent`/`p_kick_given_intent`/`p_kick_given_no_intent`, kick power, teammate aim shares, field special-action rates `special_rate`/`special_from_intent`/`field_special_rate_far`, keeper fire rates `keeper_special_rate`/`keeper_kick_fire_holding`, and cumulative per-match tackles `failed_tackles_pm`/`successful_tackles_pm`) as mean +- SE with n. Head codes 1 (`hybrid5`) and 2 (`hybrid6`) read the kick and special columns as `2 p - 1`.
  - Aim control (is the kick aimed, or is it the running steer?), pooled over the points of all matches of a directory and written under `aim_control` beside `summary` in the JSON (each key `{mean, se, n}`: `mean` is the pooled value, `se` is None, `n` is the point count; every key is None below 20 points; `evaluate_arm.py`'s `internals.json` does not carry them): points are the `rollouts.py aim` points (outfield kicks with an open teammate in the kickable arc, already aimed or not) at a kick row k >= 1. x_k is the kick's intended steer (the aim mean `aim_mean[k, i]` of a `hybrid6` seat, else the clipped steer mean `mean[k, i, 1]`), m_prev the clipped steer mean at k - 1, need = clip(deflection / 35 deg, -1, 1). `aim_control_r2_prev` = R² of x_k on m_prev (least squares with an intercept); `aim_control_r2_gain` = R² of x_k on (m_prev, need) minus `aim_control_r2_prev`; `kick_steer_noise_sd` = SD of the sampled kick steer `act[k, i, 1]` minus x_k. The report line is `aim control (r2 prev / gain / noise)` with the point count.
- `tools/audit/rollouts.py {gk,hold,aim,credit} --trace-dir DIR [--checkpoint-dir DIR] [--max-matches N] [--workers 4] [--seed 1] [--hold-steps 15] --out OUT` → counterfactual rollouts from harness traces (re-simulates matches and skips/counts any exceeding `1e-3` u position error; JSON outputs include `shaping_mode` and `gamma`). `gk` compares (a) recorded, (b) scripted go-to-ball, and (c) inert keeper actions over 1 s at loose-ball box opportunities (40-140 u, speed < 80 u/s, >= 3 s apart, >= 15 decisions left; reports pickup plus undiscounted full step rewards including shaping, with `--checkpoint-dir` accepted for CLI symmetry but unused). `hold` verifies at the first decision point of every match that the loaded policies' `val` and `mean` match the trace within `1e-5`, clones eligible outfield holding decisions (`hold_t >= 0.08`, >= 3 s apart), forces kick `+1` at step 0 (KICK) vs `-1` for `--hold-steps` steps (HOLD, default 15, recorded as `hold_steps`), and rolls 75 (5 s) and 225 (15 s) decisions with paired HOLD - KICK differences overall, by pressure (`< 70 u`, `>= 120 u`), by canonical third, and by the holder's kick intent read from the trace at the decision (`intent_kick`, `intent_mid`, `intent_hold`, `def_intent_kick`, `def_intent_hold` for the defensive third): for a `hybrid5` or `hybrid6` team p = (mean_kick + 1) / 2 gives `kick` for p > 0.5, `hold` for p < 0.05, else `mid`; for a `gaussian4` (or scripted) team `kick` for mean_kick > 0.35, else `hold`. The `intent_kick` buckets compare kicking now with waiting at the decisions where kicking is the policy's own choice; the overall average also includes decisions where the forced kick has an aim and power the policy would not use.
  - `aim --trace-dir DIR --checkpoint-dir DIR [--max-matches N] [--workers 4] [--seed 1] [--shaping-mode legacy] --out OUT` → does aiming the real kick at an open teammate pay? Points: outfield kicks with an open candidate and a direction (census pass-option definitions) where the kicker holds the ball at the kick row k, k + 2 < trace length, >= 45 decisions after the previous point; the target is the open candidate with the smallest angle to the facing and must be within 35 deg (`kick_steer_max_deflection_deg`), else no point. Three branches from one generator seed `(seed * 1_000_003 + match_idx * 10_000 + k) & 0x7FFFFFFF`, 75 decisions: POLICY plays the recorded actions at step 0 (the real kick), AIM sets the kicker's steer to clip(deflection / 35 deg, -1, 1), AIMP also sets the kick column so the ball reaches the target at about 200 u/s (`v0 = min(v_max, sqrt(200^2 + 2 * 155 * dist))`); from step 1 both teams sample from the checkpoint. Per branch: discounted team-mean non-shaping return and full return with the gamma^h critic bootstrap at h = 15, 45, 75; kicking team in possession after decisions 15 and 30; first holder other than the kicker after decisions 2-45 (`target` / `teammate` / `opponent` / `none`); goals for and against; kick direction error against the target after step 0 (None while the ball is held). Buckets `not_aimed`, `already_aimed` (census `aimed_open`), and not-aimed by third and by intent; paired AIM - POLICY and AIMP - POLICY means with SE. Verifies the checkpoint against the trace at decision 0 like `hold`; the JSON also holds every point under `records`.
  - `credit --trace-dir DIR --checkpoint-dir DIR [--decision aim|attack] [--seeds 4] [--lambdas 0 0.5 0.8 0.9 0.95 0.99 1] [--max-matches N] [--workers 4] [--seed 1] [--shaping-mode legacy] --out OUT` → how much of a decision's true counterfactual benefit reaches the trainer's GAE advantage, and with how much noise. `--decision aim` (default): the `aim` points that are not already aimed, branches POLICY (recorded actions at decision 0) and AIM (as POLICY, the kicker's steer turned at the target). `--decision attack`: outfield kicks where the kicker holds the ball at kick row k (k + 2 < trace length), the ball is in the kicker's attacking third (canonical x > 2/3 of the pitch) and k is >= 45 decisions after the previous point; branches KICK (recorded actions at decision 0) and HOLD (as KICK, the kicker's env kick column forced to -1 for decisions 0-14). Every point runs `--seeds` seed indices j (>= 2); the pair of seed index j shares the generator seed `((seed + 7919 j) * 1_000_003 + match_idx * 10_000 + k) & 0x7FFFFFFF` (j = 0 is the `aim` seed); both teams sample from the checkpoint from decision 1 on, for 75 decisions, then V_75 of the kicker's critic (no bootstrap after a terminal step). The kicker's own reward per decision is split into `goal` (10 x goals for - goals against), `sparse` (non-shaping reward - goal), `ball` (`team_advance_pbrs_weight` x the decision's change of the kicker team's ball potential; the full change, so a goal decision that teleports the ball carries the reset swing here and its opposite in `role`, while the totals are unaffected) and `role` (shaping - ball). The estimator is the trainer's GAE at decision 0 (gamma from the config, (gamma lambda)^l weights, cut at 75 with V_75) for every lambda. Per lambda it reports S ± SE (the mean over points of the seed-averaged benefit: A_AIM - A_POLICY for aim, A_KICK - A_HOLD for attack), N (sqrt of the mean over points of the variance across seeds of the recorded branch's advantage: same state, same action, different futures), SNR = S / N, the paired SD, the variance share of goal / sparse / ball / role / critic in N², and the same five parts of S. `attack` also reports goals for and against within 5 s per branch. An empty point set gives an empty result. `--out` is a `.json` file or a directory (`rollouts.json` and `report.txt`); protected directories are refused as for `aim`.
- `tools/audit/evaluate_arm.py --snapshots DIR [DIR ...] --out OUT [--frozen-dir DIR] [--workers 10] [--tags ...] [--recensus]` → runs the harness evaluation tags (`self_sampled` n=96 seed 11, `self_mixed` n=48 seed 12, `vs_scripted_sampled` and `vs_scripted_sampled_red` n=48 seed 13, `vs_frozen` and `vs_frozen_red` n=48 seed 15; the `_red` tags run with `--swap` so team A plays the `red_*` policies) and writes census outputs (plus `internals.json` for `self_sampled`) into `OUT/<gen>/<tag>/`. With `--recensus`, recomputes `census.json`, `report.txt`, and (for `self_sampled`) `internals.json` from existing `m*.npz` traces without running the harness.
- `tools/audit/calibrate.py --anchor SEED=DIR [SEED=DIR ...] --gen GEN --json OUT [--md OUT]` → calibrates relative guard and target thresholds from >= 3 anchor seeds at `GEN`, recording per-seed values, mean, sample SD `s` (ddof=1), and `delta = max(2*s, floor)`, plus `gen`, `anchor_dirs`, and `git_commit`. `--json` is required. `gates.py` rejects a calibration whose guard tag differs from `--tag`, whose target direction differs from the registered one, whose entries have fewer than 3 anchor seeds, or whose `delta` is not finite, not positive or below its `floor`.
- `tools/audit/gates.py --arm [SEED=]DIR ... [--comparator [SEED=]DIR ...] [--tag self_sampled] [--gens ...] [--protocol fresh] [--calibration PATH] [--history SEED=PATH ...] [--exit-on-c0] [--target KEY@TAG:higher|lower:DELTA] [--json OUT] [--md OUT]` → computes guard and target gates and between-seed SDs. `fresh` is the only protocol and the default of `--protocol`; `--calibration` is required.
  - The last generation in `--gens` must equal the calibration's `gen`. Gates, per arm seed unless stated:
    - **C0 catastrophe floors**, strict, at every generation >= 200 (each keeper separately): `hold_share_pooled` < 0.05, `holder_spell_s_pooled` < 0.15, `gk_in_box_A/B` < 0.80, `gk_goal_line_A/B` > 0.60, `n_within_100_pooled` > 1.00, `cluster_ge4_pooled` > 0.30, `role_inversion_pooled` > 0.55, `goals_total` < 0.40 or > 5.0, `scoreless` > 0.70.
    - **C0L learning floor**: with T2 = mean `goal_diff` of `vs_frozen` and `vs_frozen_red`, a seed fails if T2(last) - T2(100) < 0.50. Evaluated only when generation 100 is in `--gens` and is not the last one; otherwise `passed` is `null` and the note says so. A missing tag, metric, `None` or non-finite value at generation 100 or at the last generation is an error. The comparator is not checked.
    - **G1-G5 relative guards** at the last generation against the anchor values in the calibration file.
    - **G6 takeoff**: with `--history SEED=PATH` (a `training_history.json` per arm seed), fails if more than half of the arm seeds have no takeoff (`training_progress.py --takeoff` definition, default threshold and window, self-play matches only). `passed` is `null` when any arm seed lacks a history; a history for a seed that is not an arm seed is an error. The table lists each seed's takeoff generation and the generation range of its history.
    - **Targets T1-T3** at the last generation against `--comparator` or the anchor. T2 (`goal_diff` against the frozen opponent, both pairs) is the primary target and decides; T1 (`goal_diff` against the scripted bot) is informational at gen 300; T3 (`kick_outcome_teammate`) is secondary. JSON top level has `"primary_target": "T2"`.
    - **Style targets S1-S4** (`hold_share_pooled`, `aim_teammate_share_sampled`, `kick_outcome_opponent`, `goal_no_pass_10s_share` from `self_sampled`) at the last generation when `--comparator` is given, with `delta = max(2*s, floor)` from the comparator's ddof=1 SD and verdict via `_ebe_verdict`. Missing `internals.json` or any style key fails closed; without `--comparator`, `"style_targets"` is `null`. Style targets never change gate statuses or the exit code.
  - With `--exit-on-c0` the exit status is 2 if C0, C0L or G6 has `passed` false (not evaluated does not count), else 0. Without the flag the exit status is 0 whatever the gates say.
- `tools/audit/run_compare.py --run NAME=EVAL_DIR[,EVAL_DIR...] [...] [--history NAME=PATH [...]] --gens G ... [--window 100] [--md OUT] [--json OUT]` → paired readout across runs and generations. Resolves each `(run, gen)` to the first `EVAL_DIR` containing `gen<g:04d>/`, reports T1-T3, S1-S4, census and internals metrics (mean +- SE), and averages `fire_sat`, `p_kick`, `p_spec` and `v_corr_mc` per role (labelled as under "Team labels") over `[g - window + 1, g]` from `training_history.json`. Missing generations or files print `-`.
- `tools/audit/training_progress.py --bucket 100 --last-gens 15 [--takeoff [--takeoff-goals 1.5] [--takeoff-window 25]] [--fire]` → per-bucket interest/goals/passes/shots/tackles/saves/loose%/stationary over self-play matches only. Pool matches (opponent snapshot pool, `train_ppo --pool-envs`; match record `pool` set) are excluded from every statistic and from the takeoff: each bucket row counts them in `n_pooled` (printed as `pooled=K`, and in the header as `(K pool matches excluded)`, only when K > 0), a bucket with pool matches only prints `(no self-play match)`, and `--takeoff` adds `takeoff: self-play matches only (K pool matches excluded)` and fails on a generation with pool matches only. `--takeoff` prints `takeoff_gen=<g> (trailing <window>-gen mean training goals >= <goals>)` or `takeoff_gen=none (not reached by gen <last>)`: the smallest generation whose trailing window mean of the per-generation mean training-match `goals` reaches the threshold, over the whole history (every generation needs at least one match); `--json` gains a `takeoff` block. `--fire` prints per bucket the mean `fire_sat` (kick, special), `p_kick` and `p_spec` of the four roles, labelled as described under "Team labels" below (`fire: n/a (no hybrid5 telemetry)` for a history without them); `--json` gains a `fire` entry per bucket. Without the flags the output is unchanged.
- Team labels in readouts (`training_progress --fire`, `run_compare`): team A (seats 0-5, stored as `blue_field` and `blue_keeper`) is drawn red and prints as `A/red field` and `A/red keeper`; team B (stored as `red_field` and `red_keeper`) is drawn blue and prints as `B/blue field` and `B/blue keeper`. JSON and history keys keep the stored names.
- `tools/audit/replay_metrics.py --last 8` (or `--match ID`, `--gen G`, paths) → possession, width/depth, clustering, inversion, GK stats.
- `tools/audit/frame_inspect.py --match 13204 --frames 760 5531 [--viewer]` → full per-player state; `--viewer` = viewer frame counter.
- `tools/audit/retreat_probe.py --checkpoint-dir DIR [--seeds 11 12 13] [--rewards-override KEY=VALUE ...] [--json OUT]` → plays one match per seed, then reports the share of out-of-line covering defenders whose potential falls when they step 20 u back (overall / out of / in possession) and a cluster proxy from best-response ascent of out-of-possession outfielders. Overrides apply to the evaluation only, so before/after runs see the same states.
- `tools/audit/orphan_scan.py` → unread config fields, write-only attributes, unused aliases, duplicate-valued config fields.
- `tools/audit/perf_profile.py --steps 300 --seed 0 [--no-record]` → per-component ms/step and numpy calls/step.
- Every script takes `--json OUT` for full detail; stdout stays a short summary. Nothing writes to replays or source.
- Workflow: `run_all.py --save-baseline` before a change, `run_all.py` after it; read the delta column.
- Tests: `.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_audit_tools.py -q`.
